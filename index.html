Why both numbers

This question has a closed form, so the headline figure is not estimated at all. The chance that no day holds 3 or more birthdays is M! / 365^M times the coefficient of x^M in (1 + x + x²/2! + … + x^(N-1)/(N-1)!)^365. The page multiplies that polynomial out directly — exact to roughly 14 digits, in about a millisecond.

Why simulate anyway

The simulation is kept because it shows what the formula hides: how long a random experiment takes to agree, and how wide the error stays until it does. At 1,000 trials the estimate is still worth about ±3 points. Sampling error shrinks with the square root of the trial count, so each extra digit of precision costs a hundred times the work.
